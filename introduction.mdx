NDAPI (Nolubz Dedicated API Payment Infrastructure) gives your business direct access to Nolubz payment rails through one API. Collect payments through virtual accounts, verify bank account details, send bank transfers, and receive real-time webhook notifications for every event.

Whether you run a trading platform, a wallet, an escrow service, a school fee portal or any payment-enabled product, NDAPI provides the collection, payout and reconciliation layer so you can focus on your product.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a virtual account">
    Generate a unique virtual account for each customer or each transaction, and share the account number with your customer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your customer pays">
    Your customer sends a bank transfer to the virtual account. The payment is received in real time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A webhook fires instantly">
    NDAPI sends a `payment.received` webhook to your server with the full transaction details, including your own reference, so you can credit your customer immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Withdraw your balance">
    When you're ready to settle, send a bank transfer from your NDAPI balance to any supported bank account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Getting access

Access to NDAPI takes less than 24 hours.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request access">
    Fill in the [Request access form](https://ndapi.nolubz.com/get-access) with your business details and what you're building. We reply within one business day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign the agreement">
    We send you the NDAPI Service Agreement with your fee structure and terms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your credentials">
    Within 24 hours you receive your test API key, live API key and webhook secret.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    Build and test with your `NDAPI-TEST-` key, then switch to your `NDAPI-LIVE-` key when you're ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Fees

NDAPI fees are agreed individually with each operator during onboarding. There are no setup costs. New businesses can [request access](https://ndapi.nolubz.com/get-access); existing operators can ask about pricing through [operator support](https://ndapi.nolubz.com/support).
