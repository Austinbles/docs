---
title: "Transactions and Amounts"
description: "How the amount fields work on deposits and transfers."
---

Every deposit and every transfer is recorded as a transaction. All amount fields use the same meaning across API responses, transaction records and webhook payloads.

## Amount fields

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `amount` | The gross amount of the transaction. |
| `fee` | The NDAPI fee, as agreed in your service agreement. |
| `net_amount` | Deposits: `amount - fee`. Transfers: equal to `amount`. |
| `provider_fee` | Transfers only: the bank transfer charge. |
| `total_deducted` | Transfers only: `amount + fee + provider_fee`. |
| `currency` | The currency code of the transaction. |

## Deposits

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `amount` | What the payer sent into the virtual account. |
| `net_amount` | What is credited to your NDAPI balance. |

<Tip>
  Credit your customer with `net_amount`. Use `amount` for display.
</Tip>

## Transfers

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `amount` | What the recipient receives, in full. |
| `net_amount` | Same as `amount`. |
| `total_deducted` | What leaves your balance: `amount + fee + provider_fee`. |

<Note>
  Your balance must cover `total_deducted`.
</Note>

## Fees

Fee rates are agreed individually with each operator and are listed in your service agreement. Transfers between Nolubz accounts are free.

See the [Transactions API](/api-reference/transactions).
