---
title: "Virtual Accounts"
description: "Give each customer or transaction its own account number for collecting payments."
---

A virtual account is a real bank account number that routes payments into your NDAPI balance. When a customer pays into it, NDAPI credits your balance and sends a `payment.received` webhook carrying your reference.

## Permanent and dynamic accounts

| Type | Expiry | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Permanent | Never expires | Wallet funding, recurring payments, one account per customer |
| Dynamic | Expires at `expires_at` | One-time payments, checkout, P2P trades |

Leave `expires_at` empty for a permanent account.

## Account name

The account name includes your business name: `{your business name}/{account_name}`, for example `YourBusiness/John Doe`. Customers see it in their banking app before paying.

## Matching payments

Set `merchant_tx_ref` to your own order or customer ID. NDAPI returns it as `reference` in every webhook and transaction record, so you always know where a payment belongs.

## Expected amount

`expected_amount` is optional and informational. If the customer sends a different amount, the payment is still accepted and your balance is credited with the amount actually received. Always credit your customer from the webhook's `net_amount`, never from the amount you expected.

See the [Virtual Accounts API](/api-reference/virtual-accounts).
