---
title: "Transfers"
description: "Send payouts from your NDAPI balance to bank accounts."
---

A transfer sends money from your NDAPI balance to a bank account. The recipient always receives the full `amount`. NDAPI charges are taken from your balance on top, so you stay in control of how you bill your own users:

```
net_amount     = amount                        (what the recipient receives)
total_deducted = amount + fee + provider_fee   (what leaves your balance)
```

Your balance must cover `total_deducted`.

## Transfer status

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `completed` | Funds delivered to the recipient. |
| `pending` | Accepted and awaiting processing. Watch for `transfer.success` or `transfer.failed`. |
| `failed` | The transfer failed and your balance was restored automatically. |

## Before you send

Always verify the account name first with [Verify Account](/api-reference/virtual-accounts#verify-a-bank-account), and confirm it with your user.

## Safe retries

Every transfer needs a unique `reference`. If a request times out, retry with the **same** reference. A transfer is never processed twice. See [Error handling](/guides/error-handling#idempotency).

See the [Transfers API](/api-reference/transfers).
